RuneBags Rules

1. Goal

Win points by creating winning lines of 4 or more contiguous runes. The game revolves around a shared 10-point supply.

2. Core Win and Points

A round ends immediately when a player forms a winning line of at least 4. That player takes 1 point from supply.

If both players form a winning line from the same resolved action, the round is a draw.

If a round is tied, 1 point is removed from supply instead of awarded.

A player wins the game by majority of remaining obtainable points. If supply reaches 0, highest score wins. If scores are tied, fewer runes in bag wins.

3. Round, Shop, and Turn Flow

The game starts in Shop Phase.

After shop, a new round starts. Starter by parity: even supply means White starts, odd means Black starts.

When a round ends, you enter Shop Phase before the next round.

4. Hands and Visibility

Hands are pass-and-play hidden by default. Active player hand is shown during round turns.

If Uruz is on board for one side, opponent hand is forced visible.

5. Neutral Runes

Neutral runes are gray blockers. They are not Black or White ownership.

They do not score by themselves, do not return to player bags, and return to neutral supply when removed or at round settlement.

6. Forced Pass

If a player has no legal playable rune, they must pass.

If both players cannot play, the round is a draw.

7. Shop Phase

Each player reveals 5 runes from their personal shop supply and may add up to 2 to their bag.

Shop actions in any order:

- Remove 1 rune once per shop phase: basic removed permanently, neutral to supply, symbol rune returns to that player's shop supply.

- Combine 2 matching level-1 runes into one level-2 rune (except non-combinable runes).

- Add up to 2 runes from the 5 offered.

8. Rune Effects

Inguz: center column only.

Jera: edge columns only.

Laguz: cannot be moved or removed; Algiz cannot be played in any column containing Laguz.

Algiz: inserts from column bottom and pushes upward.

Ansuz: returns rune directly beneath to owner bag (not neutral).

Berkana: if in owner winning line (4+), grants +1 bonus point once for that round, even with multiple Berkanas.

Ehwaz: increases owner max hand size while on board.

Gebo: removes rune for the round (L2 chooses adjacent target).

Hagalz: adjacent neutral runes can count for owner wins, one Hagalz per converted neutral in a line.

Isa: remains on board across rounds.

Mannaz: adds neutral rune to opponent bag; level 1 is ethereal.

Odal: if played on the top row (rank 6), owner gains 1 point from supply without automatically ending the round.

Perth: constrains opponent next turn to adjacent columns; level 2 lets you choose the adjacent forced column.

Raido: grants extra turn; level 1 is ethereal.

Sowelu: discards random rune(s) from opponent bag for the round.

Teiwaz: move top rune (adjacent at L1, any column at L2).

Thurisa: place extra neutral rune(s) from supply.

Uruz: opponent hand cannot be hidden while active.

9. Ethereal and Visual Cues

Ethereal runes use dashed styling and return to shop supply instead of player bag at round settlement.

Winning lines and forced columns are highlighted on board, including full 4+ combo lines and both sides in simultaneous-win draws.
